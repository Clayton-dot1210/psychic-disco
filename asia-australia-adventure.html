Good to Know

A few helpful tips so we can focus on the fun

  • Japan: March–May for cherry blossoms; October–November for autumn colours.
  • Thailand & Vietnam: November–March for dry weather and cooler temperatures.
  • Bali: April–October is dry season — perfect beach weather.
  • Australia: September–November (spring) is ideal — warm, not too hot, fewer crowds.
  • Eat at hawker centres and street stalls — the food is often better and always cheaper.
  • Book flights 3–4 months ahead for the best prices, especially long-haul.
  • Get a local SIM card on arrival — it's fast, cheap and essential.
  • Carry small cash for markets and tuk-tuks; cards for hotels.
  • Stay hydrated — tropical heat is deceptively draining.
  • High-factor sunscreen every day, even when it's cloudy.
  • Check if any vaccinations are needed well in advance (Hep A, typhoid, Japanese encephalitis).
  • Travel insurance that covers adventure activities is a must.

Pack light — you'll want space for souvenirs! Breathable linen and cotton clothes are your best friend in the heat. A lightweight rain jacket folds to almost nothing but saves the day. Comfortable walking shoes that don't look like hiking boots are worth their weight in gold.

  • Book a private villa in Bali for at least a few nights — they're surprisingly affordable and utterly magical.
  • Pre-book a sunset dinner cruise in Sydney Harbour.
  • Watch the Hội An lantern festival on the 14th of each lunar month.
  • A couples' Balinese massage is a non-negotiable joy.
  • Kyoto at dawn — before the crowds — is the most romantic place on earth.
